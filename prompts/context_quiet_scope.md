Quiet-scope contract (ordinary extraction and mixed clarification answers):
quiet_hours controls notifications to the owner, not everybody in the household.
When emitting quiet_hours=true, include the metadata quiet_hours_basis beside it
inside the flags object (or beside it in the ordinary flat output):
- "owner_request": the owner currently asks for quiet or no interruptions.
- "shared_rest": current rest or sleep involves the owner or a child sharing
  the owner's present environment, so notifications would disturb that rest.
- "others_rest": only other people are resting elsewhere; omit quiet_hours.
Without a grounded owner scope, omit quiet_hours and its basis. Never assume
that sleeping relatives at home need the owner silenced on a separate journey,
at work or elsewhere. Future bedtime alone establishes no current silence.
An explicit owner quiet request still applies outside the home. A clear request
to resume notifications may emit quiet_hours=false without basis metadata.
The basis is output-validation metadata, not a writable context-state flag.

Resolve each subject and location separately. Family remaining at home does not
place the owner there. A completed personal departure with relatives explicitly
remaining home establishes separation; it does not establish arrival at work.
Preserve explicit co-presence in joint travel and do not invent whereabouts.
